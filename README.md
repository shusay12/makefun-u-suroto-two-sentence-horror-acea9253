Entry for the [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one horror story of exactly two sentences to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The new tenants asked why the previous family never took their daughter's drawings off the walls. I told them she drew only what she saw — and the newest one, dated yesterday, shows the two of them asleep in the bedroom upstairs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical files (see `run1/entry.txt` and `run2/entry.txt`).
